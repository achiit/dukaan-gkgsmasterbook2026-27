Q.2 Which Article deals with the Right to Constitutional Remedies?

  1. Article 19
  2. Article 21
  3. Article 32
  4. Article 44
Correct Answer: C) Article 32
Explanation: Article 32 lets citizens approach the Supreme Court directly to enforce Fundamental Rights. / अनुच्छेद 32 से सीधे सर्वोच्च न्यायालय जाया जा सकता है।

  1. Joule
  2. Newton
  3. Pascal

Acids, Bases & pH / अम्ल, क्षार
  • pH < 7 acidic; 7 neutral; > 7 basic
  • Blue litmus → red in acid; red litmus → blue in base
  • Lemon — citric acid; Tamarind — tartaric acid; Curd — lactic acid; Ant sting — formic acid
